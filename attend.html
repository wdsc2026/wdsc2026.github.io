Who should attend

Researchers building pieces of the same system.

WDSC is designed to create interaction among communities that often solve connected problems using different models, assumptions, and implementation stacks.

01

Cryptography researchers

Threshold and distributed cryptography, MPC, anonymous authentication, post-quantum protocols, ZK proofs, verifiable computation, and encrypted data systems.

02

Systems builders

Wallets, custody, ledgers, decentralized identity, privacy infrastructure, audit systems, and access-controlled collaboration.

03

Students & early-career researchers

Short submissions and focused discussion make the workshop suitable for emerging work, new connections, and concrete open problems.
