Organizers

Research across the full collaboration stack.

SC

Sherman S. M. Chow

The Chinese University of Hong Kong

Associate Professor in Information Engineering. His research covers cryptography, privacy, secure distributed systems, threshold signatures, anonymous authentication, searchable and access-control encryption, zero-knowledge proofs, and decentralized cryptosystems.

smchow [at] ie.cuhk.edu.hkInstitution
SG

Shang Gao

Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Assistant Professor with a joint appointment in Computing and Accounting & Finance. His work develops efficient zero-knowledge proofs and recursive SNARKs for anonymous blockchain systems and privacy-preserving Web3 applications.

shanggao [at] polyu.edu.hkInstitution
HW

Harry W. H. Wong

National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University

Assistant Professor in Computer Science and director of the Applied Cryptography Lab. His work includes MPC, threshold cryptography, distributed signing, privacy-preserving authentication, lattice-based schemes, and real-world deployment.

harrywong [at] nycu.edu.twInstitution
YY

Yingfei Yan

University Clermont Auvergne

Postdoctoral researcher working on lattice-based privacy-preserving protocols, zero-knowledge proofs, private signatures, anonymous authentication and tokens, and succinct arguments.

yingfei.yan [at] uca.frInstitution
