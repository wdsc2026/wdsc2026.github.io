Call for Sponsors

WDSC 2026 welcomes expressions of interest from academic institutions, research laboratories, foundations, and industry organizations that support research in cryptography, privacy, and secure systems.

Subject to ASIACRYPT 2026 policies and organizer approval, sponsorship may be used to broaden participation, including possible partial registration or local-travel support for students, early-career researchers, or invited academic speakers.

To discuss sponsorship: contact any organizer listed above and use the subject line “WDSC 2026 Sponsorship”.

01

Academic independence

Sponsorship will not influence submission review, speaker selection, accepted presentations, or the technical program.

02

Appropriate recognition

Any acknowledgement will be agreed with the ASIACRYPT 2026 organizers. Sponsored technical slots, commercial advertisements, banners, and merchandise are not part of the workshop format.
