Call for presentations

Bring a result,
a system,
or a hard question.

We invite non-archival submissions on cryptographic mechanisms for decentralized secure collaboration.

Abstract deadline

15September
2026 Submission time zone to be confirmed.

Modern collaboration systems increasingly operate without a single trusted controller. WDSC asks how cryptography can support such collaboration while preserving privacy, robustness, accountability, and public verifiability.

Submissions may describe new technical results, ongoing work, implementation experience, negative results, standardization or migration gaps, open problems, or position statements. The goal is technical discussion across adjacent communities rather than a new proceedings venue.

Non-archival

Previously published, concurrently submitted, or ongoing work is welcome when its status is clearly disclosed and a citation or link is provided where available.

Topics of interest

Mechanisms, systems, migration, and deployment.

Relevant topics include, but are not limited to, the areas below.

01

Control & authorization

  • Threshold signatures and distributed authorization
  • MPC signing and distributed key generation
  • Key recovery, rotation, and proactive refresh
  • Wallet, custody, PKI, ledger, and governance protocols
02

Authentication & privacy

  • Anonymous authentication and credentials
  • Private tokens, reputation, and revocation
  • Abuse-resistant participation
  • Post-quantum migration for signatures, credentials, tokens, and authentication
03

Verification & accountability

  • Zero-knowledge proofs and SNARKs
  • Proof aggregation and verifiable computation
  • Auditability and public verification without disclosure
  • Encrypted sharing, searchable encryption, and access-controlled collaboration
↗

Cross-cutting experience

  • Implementations, benchmarks, and APIs
  • Interoperability and deployment lessons
  • Negative results and failure modes
  • Standardization gaps and open problems

Submission format

Concise, identifiable, and discussion-ready.

01

PDF abstract

At most three pages, plus references. A short abstract or talk proposal is sufficient.

02

Non-anonymous

List all authors and clearly identify the author who will deliver the presentation.

03

Optional context

You may include a full paper, expected slides, software or project links, or a recording of a related talk.

04

Transparent status

Disclose whether the work is ongoing, submitted elsewhere, accepted, or previously published.

Frequently asked questions

What “non-archival” means here.

Will WDSC publish proceedings?

No. The workshop will not publish proceedings, and accepted abstracts are not intended to become archival publications.

May I present work that is submitted or published elsewhere?

Yes. Clearly disclose the status and include a citation or link when available.

What happens after acceptance?

Accepted contributions will be scheduled as short talks, posters, or discussion prompts depending on submission volume and program balance. The identified presenter is expected to deliver the contribution at the workshop.

Will titles and abstracts appear online?

With author permission, accepted talk titles and abstracts may be listed on this workshop website.
