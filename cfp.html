Call for presentations

Call for presentations

We invite short, non-archival contributions on cryptography for secure collaboration without a single trusted controller.

Abstract submission: 01 October 2026
Workshop: Monday, 7 December 2026 · Hong Kong

What belongs here

About submissions

We welcome new results, work in progress, implementation experience, negative results, migration gaps, open problems, and position statements.

Non-archival

Previously published, concurrently submitted, and ongoing work is welcome when its status is clearly disclosed.

Topics

Topics

01Control

Threshold signatures, MPC signing, key management, wallets, and governance.

02Privacy

Anonymous authentication, credentials, private tokens, revocation, and abuse resistance.

03Verification

Zero-knowledge proofs, SNARKs, verifiable computation, and auditability.

04Deployment

Post-quantum migration, interoperability, APIs, benchmarks, and failure modes.

Submission

Submission format

Submit a non-anonymous PDF abstract of at most three pages plus references. Name all authors and identify the presenter.

Format PDF abstract, up to three pages plus references

Contribution Short talk, poster, or discussion prompt

Status Disclose ongoing, submitted, accepted, or published work
